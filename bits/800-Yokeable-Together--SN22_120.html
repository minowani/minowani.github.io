Yokeable Together (SN 22.120)

Saṃyutta Nikāya
Khandhavaggo
Khandhasaṃyuttaṃ
Dhammakathikavaggo
Saṃyojaniyasuttaṃ

Situated at Sāvatthi.

'I shall point out, almsmen, and yokeable together principles and the yoking together. Hear that.

And which, almsmen, yokeable together principles? which the yoking together?

  • Form, almsmen, a yokeable together principle; what there the interest plus passion is, that there the yoking together.
  • Feeling, almsmen, a yokeable together principle; what there the interest plus passion is, that there the yoking together.
  • Perception, almsmen, a yokeable together principle; what there the interest plus passion that there the yoking together.
  • Inclinations, almsmen, are a yokeable together principle; what there the interest plus passion is, that there the yoking together.
  • Cognition, almsmen, a yokeable together principle; what there the interest plus passion is, that there the yoking together.

These are called, almsmen, yokeable together principles; this the yoking together.'
